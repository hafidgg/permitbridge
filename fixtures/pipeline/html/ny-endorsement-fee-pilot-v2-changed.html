Endorsement of Nursing Licenses - RPN

How do I apply for Licensure by Endorsement?

If you are licensed as an R.N. or L.P.N. in another state and have completed your professional nursing education in another state, you must submit the following:

  • Form 1 - Application for Licensure* along with the $160 fee;
  • Form 2 - Certification of Professional Education; and
  • Form 3 - Verification of Other Professional Licensure/Certification or Nursys online verification of licensure for all other states where a license is held.

*Additional coursework in Child Abuse Reporting and/or Infection Control may be required.
