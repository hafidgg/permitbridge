## Who Licenses Plumbers in Texas

The Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners (TSBPE) licenses plumbers in Texas. This guide draws on two of its documents: the board's compilation of its administrative rules (March 2026), which covers Title 22, Chapter 363 ("Examination and Registration"), and its Out-of-State Examination Application (revised March 2026).

The compilation lists these sections under Chapter 363:

- §363.4 Master Plumber License
- §363.5 Journeyman Plumber License
- §363.6 Tradesman Plumber-Limited License
- §363.7 Plumber's Apprentice Registration

The requirements for each are below, with the section they come from. Several sections refer to an RMP, the compilation's abbreviation for a Responsible Master Plumber.

## Plumber's Apprentice Registration (§363.7)

§363.7 has a single requirement: "To be eligible for a Plumber's Apprentice Registration an applicant must be at least sixteen (16) years of age."

## Tradesman Plumber-Limited License (§363.6)

Under §363.6(a), an applicant must:

- complete "twenty-four (24) hours of approved training provided by an approved instructor in an approved training program in the areas of health and safety, the latest version of all plumbing codes adopted by the Board, and water conservation." Those hours must include "one (1) six-hour apprentice continuing professional education class", "the OSHA 10-Hour Outreach Training class" and "the eight (8) hours of training on residential plumbing installation";
- "have at least 4,000 hours of experience working at the trade under the supervision of a RMP or a Master Plumber licensed in another state"; and
- hold one of the following: "a current Plumber's Apprentice Registration", "a current Journeyman Plumber License issued in another state" or "a current Master Plumber License issued in another state."

§363.6(b) allows "up to 1,000 hours of the work experience required to take the Tradesman Plumber-Limited examination" to be credited "if the applicant has completed a training program" approved by the U.S. Department of Labor, Office of Apprenticeship, or "provided by an approved person and based on approved course materials."

Under §363.6(d), an applicant is exempt from the 24-hour training requirement if the applicant "holds a current Master or Journeyman Plumber License issued in another state."

## Journeyman Plumber License (§363.5)

§363.5(a): "To be eligible for a Journeyman Plumber License an applicant must have completed forty-eight (48) hours of classroom training provided by an approved instructor in an approved training program". §363.5(f) says that program includes "two (2) six-hour Continuing Professional Education (CPE) classes" and the OSHA 10-Hour Outreach Training class on construction.

§363.5(b) adds two requirements:

- "have at least 8,000 hours of experience working at the trade under the supervision of a RMP or a Master Plumber licensed in another state"; and
- hold one of the following: "a current Plumber's Apprentice Registration", "a current Tradesman Plumber-Limited License" or "a current Journeyman Plumber License issued in another state."

§363.5(c) allows a credit of "up to 1,000 hours of the work experience required to take the Journeyman Plumber examination" for a completed classroom portion of an approved training program. Under §363.5(e), an applicant is exempt from the training if the applicant "holds a current Journeyman Plumber License issued in another state."

## Master Plumber License (§363.4)

§363.4 accepts a Journeyman license issued in Texas or in another state: "To be eligible for a Master Plumber License an applicant must have held a Journeyman Plumber License issued in Texas or another state: (1) for at least two years; or (2) for at least one year if the applicant has successfully completed a training program approved by the United States Department of Labor, Office of Apprenticeship or another nationally-recognized apprentice training program accepted by the Board."

## Applying From Another State: The Out-of-State Examination

TSBPE's [Out-of-State Examination Application](https://tsbpe.texas.gov/wp-content/uploads/forms/TSBPE_OutOfStateExaminationRequest.pdf) (revised March 2026) is for the Journeyman and Master licenses. It lists these "Minimum Requirements for an Out-of-State Examination":

- "Proof that the applicant is authorized to work lawfully in the United States"
- "Proof applicant is currently licensed as a Journeyman or Master Plumber in another state"
- "Must have held the above license for two (2) years"
- "Non-refundable Out-of-State Examination processing fee payable to the TSBPE: Journeyman - $40, Master – $25"

The form also says:

- **Identity:** "Current (not expired) US or state government-issued photographic identification, such as a driver license, passport, or state-issued identification, will be required from the applicant on the day of the examination."
- **Fingerprints:** "Fingerprints are required for all out-of-state examinees." The form adds: "This application will not be approved until corresponding fingerprint data is on file."
- **Exam format:** according to the form, the Journeyman and Master out-of-state exams are computer-based and administered by Pearson VUE. A footnote adds: "The Journeyman exam also includes a practical exam portion."
- **Waiting periods after a failed exam:** "(1) First failure: a 30-day waiting period, (2) Second failure, a 60-day waiting period, (3) Third and subsequent failures: a 90-day waiting period."
- **Expiry:** "Incomplete applications expire one year from receipt."

The form describes the qualifying license in two different ways. The minimum requirements say "Proof applicant is currently licensed as a Journeyman or Master Plumber in another state". The checklist asks for a "Legible copy of the applicant's current, state-issued Residential, Journeyman or Master plumbing license". The form does not say whether a Residential plumbing license alone qualifies. Ask TSBPE before you apply on that basis.

## The Louisiana Agreement

TSBPE has a page titled "TSBPE Signs Reciprocal License Agreement with Louisiana", dated January 11, 2022. Its headline reads: "Louisiana Journeymen with Gasfitter Licensees in Good Standing are Now Eligible to Obtain the Equivalent License in Texas, No Exam Required". The page does not describe an agreement with any other state. If you hold a Louisiana license, read the [page itself](https://tsbpe.texas.gov/louisianareciprocity/) and confirm with TSBPE that your license fits its wording.

## How to Use This Guide

1. Find the license you want in the sections above. Note the rule section it comes from.
2. Compare each requirement in that section with your own training, experience and current license.
3. If you hold a Journeyman or Master license from another state, read the Out-of-State Examination section next.
4. Open the board's documents from the Sources list. Check that the versions match the ones this guide names, and rely on the documents if they differ.

## Questions to Ask TSBPE Before You Apply

- Which license should I apply for, given the license I hold now?
- Does my out-of-state license exempt me from the training requirement in the rule section for that license?
- Can training I have completed be credited toward the required hours of experience?
- Does a Residential plumbing license from my state meet the Out-of-State Examination requirements?
- Has my fingerprint data already been submitted?
- If I hold a Louisiana license, does it match the wording of the Louisiana agreement?
