## What This Guide Covers

This guide sets out what the Illinois Department of Financial and Professional Regulation (IDFPR) publishes about licensure by endorsement for registered nurses who already hold a license in another U.S. state: the fee, the temporary permit, license verification, the education form, fingerprinting, and the time limits. It draws on IDFPR's Registered Nurse application packet (RN-EN, instructions revised 4/24, packet updated 10/17/24) and its nursing page.

It does not cover licensure by examination, restoration of a lapsed Illinois license, advanced practice (APRN) licensure, or the full requirements for nurses educated outside the U.S. It is not legal advice.

## What the Board's Own Text Says

### Fees

The packet's instruction sheet lists "Endorsement - $50.00" and "Temporary Permit - $25.00". Its reference sheet states: "ALL FEES ARE NONREFUNDABLE". If you apply for both at once, the packet says to "Combine the $50 endorsement fee and the $25 temporary permit fee into one check or money order for $75". Fee payment "must be in the form of a check or money order made payable to Department of Financial and Professional Regulation".

### Who can apply

The temporary permit instructions state: "Current licensure in at least one other jurisdiction of the United States is required by the Illinois Nursing and Advanced Practice Nursing Act". The packet defines endorsement as: "Original license issued in another state and that state's requirements were substantially equivalent to Illinois requirements at time license was issued."

The packet singles out one program: "Excelsior College is an unapproved nursing education program in the State of Illinois". It adds that graduates may still be reviewed individually "provided the applicant is currently licensed in another U.S. jurisdiction and has been actively practicing in clinical nursing for a minimum of two (2) years." Those applicants must submit a VE (Verification of Employment) form "verifying two full years of clinical practice as a registered nurse."

### What to submit

For nurses educated in the United States or its territories, the packet lists:

- "Application for Licensure and/or Examination (four page)"
- "Supporting Document PHQ must be completed and submitted with each application. Your application will not be processed without completion of this form"
- CT-NUR (Verification of Licensing Agency/Board): "Submit verification of licensure from the state of original licensure, current state of licensure and any jurisdiction in which you have actively practiced within the last 5 years"
- ED-NUR (Certificate of Education), signed by the director of your nursing program with the school seal, "OR official transcripts with school seal affixed"
- The fee

### License verification through Nursys

The packet states: "If the state(s) where you have been licensed as a nurse participates in Nursys®, you must request verification of your licensure through Nursys® (www.nursys.com), not the state(s)." If your state does not appear on the Nursys list of participating boards, "you must use the CT-NUR form (Verification of Licensing Agency/Board) to verify your license to the Illinois Board of Nursing."

### Fingerprints

The instruction sheet states: "All applicants for initial licensure must submit to a criminal background check and provide evidence of fingerprint processing from the Illinois State Police or its designated agent." The packet's background check notice adds: "Fingerprints must be taken within 60 days from the date that the application is submitted to the Department or the Department's testing vendor." Applicants fingerprinted in Illinois submit "a copy of the fingerprint receipt"; those fingerprinted elsewhere submit "a completed OOS-FP form".

### Temporary permit

The packet states: "you may be eligible to receive a temporary permit. The permit is valid for six months from the date of issuance, or issuance of an Illinois Registered Nurse License, or notification that the Department intends to deny licensure, whichever comes first." It adds: "It will be your responsibility to complete the endorsement licensure process prior to the expiration of the temporary permit."

To request the permit, the packet lists the application, the PHQ supporting document, the TP-NUR form, "Copies of all current active Registered/Licensed Practical Nurse licenses and/or temporary permits/licenses held by you in any other jurisdiction(s) of the United States", the combined $75 fee, and proof of fingerprint submission.

### Time limits and renewal

The instruction sheet states: "You have three years from the date your application is received by the Department or Continental Testing Service, Inc. to complete the application process. If the process is not completed in three years, your application will be denied and the fee forfeited."

On renewal: "All Registered Nurses licenses expire on May 31 of every even-number year, regardless of the date of issuance."

## Steps, as the Forms Describe Them

1. Confirm you hold a current RN license in at least one other U.S. jurisdiction.
2. Request license verification through Nursys if your state participates; otherwise send the CT-NUR form to each required board.
3. Send the ED-NUR form to your nursing program, or arrange official transcripts with the school seal.
4. Get fingerprinted within 60 days of submitting your application, and keep the receipt or complete the OOS-FP form.
5. Complete the four-page application and the PHQ supporting document.
6. If you want a temporary permit, add the TP-NUR form and copies of your current licenses, and pay $75 instead of $50.
7. Mail the documents to IDFPR's Division of Professional Regulation, or use the online endorsement application linked from IDFPR's nursing page, and complete the process within three years.

## What This Guide Cannot Tell You

Confirm these with IDFPR before you apply:

- How long IDFPR takes to process an endorsement application. The packet does not state a processing time.
- Whether the online application has different document steps from the paper packet.
- Whether your license history raises a question in the PHQ supporting document that requires extra documentation.
- What the fingerprint vendor will charge. The packet tells out-of-state applicants to "Contact the vendor to determine the fee".
