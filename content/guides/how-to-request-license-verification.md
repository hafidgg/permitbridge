## Why Verification Comes First

When you apply in a new state, the receiving board checks that your existing license is real, active and in good standing. This step is called license verification, and each board we cite below asks for it.

## Step 1: Identify the Verification Method Your Profession Uses

Some professions verify through a national registry: nursing uses [Nursys](https://www.nursys.com/). New York's board describes it this way: "The National Council of State Boards of Nursing (NCSBN) handles verification of licensure for a majority of states through their Nurse System (Nursys)." Source: [NYSED endorsement page](https://www.op.nysed.gov/professions/registered-professional-nursing/endorsement-nursing-licenses). Trade boards may handle it differently: Texas's TDLR tells Master Electrician applicants to "contact the state where you are licensed to request a letter of good standing", while for Journeyman Electrician applicants "The Department will contact the state licensing authority" (below).

## Step 2: Request Early

Request verification before you submit your full application to the new state. Verification is handled by your current state's board or by Nursys, separately from the receiving state's own review, and how long it takes depends on that board. The Texas Board of Nursing, for example, states: "Licensure verification requests are answered within 10 working days." Source: [Texas BON Compact with Texans](https://www.bon.texas.gov/compact_with_texans.asp.html).

## What the Boards We Cite Actually Ask For

The exact document differs by board. These are the verification requirements as each board states them on the page we cite:

- **Texas, electricians (TDLR).** For a Master Electrician license by reciprocity: "You must contact the state where you are licensed to request a letter of good standing, which must show that you have passed the statewide examination for electrical work." For a Journeyman Electrician license: "The Department will contact the state licensing authority to certify the following: you have passed the statewide examination for electrical work in that state, you are licensed as a Journeyman Electrician, and that your license is in good standing." Source: [TDLR out-of-state electrician page](https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/electricians/out-of-state.htm).
- **New York, registered nurses (NYSED).** If you are licensed in another state and "have completed your professional nursing education in another state, you must submit the following: Form 1 - Application for Licensure" (with "the $143 fee"), "Form 2 - Certification of Professional Education", and "Form 3 - Verification of Other Professional Licensure/Certification or Nursys online verification of licensure for all other states where a license is held." Source: [NYSED endorsement page](https://www.op.nysed.gov/professions/registered-professional-nursing/endorsement-nursing-licenses).
- **California, registered nurses (BRN).** BRN's endorsement application checklist asks for a "Completed Verification of License form" (or a Nursys verification if your board of nursing participates in Nursys) and "Transcripts sent directly from your school of nursing." Source: [BRN Licensure by Endorsement application instructions](https://www.rn.ca.gov/pdfs/applicants/end-app.pdf).
- **Illinois, registered nurses (IDFPR).** License verification through Nursys when your current state participates in it. Source: [IDFPR RN endorsement application](https://idfpr.illinois.gov/content/dam/soi/en/web/idfpr/renewals/apply/forms/RN-EN.pdf).

Two patterns show up across these: the three nursing boards accept Nursys verification, and the Texas trade board either has the applicant request a "letter of good standing" (Master Electrician) or contacts the licensing state itself (Journeyman Electrician).

## Step 3: Track the Delivery Method

Some documents go directly to the receiving board rather than through you; California's BRN, for example, states: "If you attended a nursing program in another State, you may have your transcripts sent to our Board electronically from a certified third-party electronic transcripts vendor such as Parchment, National Student Clearinghouse" … "or directly from your school." Source: [BRN licensure by endorsement](https://www.rn.ca.gov/applicants/lic-end). Confirm whether you need to give the receiving board's mailing address or portal ID as part of the request.

## Step 4: Follow Up Before Assuming It's Lost

If the receiving board hasn't confirmed that your verification arrived, contact it to check, and ask your current board to confirm it was sent.
