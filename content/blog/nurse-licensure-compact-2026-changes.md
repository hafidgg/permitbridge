## Where the Compact Stands in 2026

The Nurse Licensure Compact continues to add member states, though implementation timelines vary — some newly-enacted states have passed legislation but not yet begun issuing multistate licenses.

## What Changed for Traveling and Relocating Nurses

For nurses relocating between two compact states, the multistate privilege continues to remove the need for a brand-new application in most cases. For nurses moving to or from a non-compact state, licensure by endorsement remains the standard path.

## Where to Check Current Status

Because implementation dates differ from enactment dates, always confirm current multistate licensure status directly with your state's board of nursing or the NLC's official compact map before relying on it for an upcoming move.
