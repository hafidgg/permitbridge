## Mid-Year Snapshot

As of mid-2026, [a majority of US states have enacted some form of Universal License Recognition law](https://compacts.csg.org/understanding-arizonas-universal-occupational-licensing-recognition-bill/) — a trend that began with Arizona in 2019 and has grown steadily since — though the scope and strength of these laws still vary widely: some cover nearly all occupational licenses, while others exclude healthcare or legal professions entirely.

## States to Watch

Several additional states have proposed ULR legislation currently moving through committee. We track enactment dates directly on each state's page rather than in this post, since a running list here would go stale within weeks.

## What This Means for Applicants

If you're planning a move, check your destination state's page on PermitBridge for its current ULR status before assuming older guidance still applies — this is exactly the kind of detail that changes fastest.
